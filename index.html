First — what is a patient advocate?

A patient advocate is an independent professional who works only for you. Not the hospital, not the insurance company — you. It is a real and growing field, but most people never hear about it until they are already in the middle of something hard.

The work is practical. We read the fine print in your plan, challenge denied claims with written appeals, find and correct billing errors, negotiate balances with providers, help with Medicare and Medicaid paperwork, and translate what the system is telling you into plain language. You keep every decision. We carry the paperwork, the phone calls, and the follow-up.

Why a person, not a free AI appeal tool?

  • A human can call your insurer and negotiate on your behalf — not just draft a letter.
  • Complex, high-stakes, or multi-provider cases need judgment a template can't give.
  • We manage the case over weeks, through every reply and appeal level.
  • Someone local and accountable, who has done this work from inside the system.
What we do

Services

Coastal Healthcare Advocates offers a range of services to help you understand your medical bills, resolve insurance issues, and get the most out of your benefits.

Insurance Claim Denial Appeals

When a claim is denied, we build the written, evidence-backed appeal — gathering records, citing your plan's own rules, and pushing it through every level of review. Typically $175–$300

Medical Bill Negotiation

We contact the provider or billing office directly to reduce an inflated balance, remove errors, or set up a realistic payment plan you can live with. Typically $125–$250

EOB Analysis

A line-by-line read of your Explanation of Benefits against the itemized bill, to catch duplicate charges, coding mistakes, and services you were billed for but never received. Bill review $125–$200

Medicare / Medicaid Claims Support

Help understanding what is covered, filing and following up on claims, and responding to denials and appeals within the Medicare and Medicaid systems. Billed hourly at $85

Debt Resolution / Financial Assistance

We help you find hospital charity-care and financial-assistance programs, dispute balances that have gone to collections, and organize medical debt into something manageable. Single-issue package from $225

Services for Professionals

For attorneys, care managers, financial planners, and clinicians: reliable claims and billing support for the clients you refer, with clear communication back to your office. Talk about referrals

Built for the person holding the paperwork

Four steps, at your pace

  1. Free consultation: A 20–30 minute phone or video call. You describe what's happening; Lindsey tells you honestly whether and how she can help — no charge, no obligation.
  2. Document collection: We gather the bills, Explanation of Benefits statements, denial letters, and plan documents that matter — through a secure channel, not the website form.
  3. Review & action plan: Lindsey reviews everything, identifies where the claim or bill went wrong, and gives you a written plan with the steps, the likely timeline, and the cost.
  4. Negotiation & follow-up: We make the calls, send the appeals, and stay on the case through every reply — keeping you updated until it's resolved or every option is exhausted.
