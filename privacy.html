1. What information do we collect?

Information you give us

When you submit the contact form, we collect the name you enter, the email address and/or phone number you provide, your preferred contact method, how soon you need help, and the free-text message. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to share in that message or call.

The contact form asks you not to include Social Security numbers, diagnoses, or other sensitive health details. Please follow that guidance — we will gather clinical and billing details securely after we speak.

Information collected automatically

When you load the site, your browser sends standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the page you are on. This is used to serve the site and keep it secure. If you accept analytics, Plausible records the page path and referrer in aggregate, without cookies and without identifying you.

2. How do we process your information?

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule a consultation.
  • To provide advocacy services if you become a client.
  • To keep records we are required to keep, and to establish or defend legal claims.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the site.
  • With your consent, to understand which pages help visitors (analytics).

3. When and with whom do we share information?

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that operate the site and our practice on our behalf — for example the form host that delivers your submission and our email provider. (TODO: name each provider and the safeguards in place.)
  • Your insurers, providers, or agencies, but only as needed to carry out the work you have asked us to do, and with your authorization.
  • Authorities or advisors where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Cookies and analytics

The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses a small amount of local browser storage to remember your cookie choice and interface preferences.

Analytics are off until you choose "Accept analytics" on the banner. We use Plausible Analytics, which is privacy-focused: no cookies, no cross-site tracking, and no collection of personal or health information. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie preferences" in the site footer. We do not currently use social-login features.
