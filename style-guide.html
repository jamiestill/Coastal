Contrast, as verified on the live pages: body/headings run ~16:1, secondary text 7–9:1, and Paper text on the Navy band and Midnight footer ≥9:1 — all clear of WCAG AA. Where one has to carry text or meaning, use --link or its -ink step (see Tokens).

Two rules that sit alongside the scale. Measure governs width — prose stops at 62ch, legal pages and FAQ answers at 68ch, ledes at 60ch — the scale only governs height. And legibility outranks density: body text never drops below 18px, and supporting text never below 14px.

The mark — "Advocate Beacon"

A lighthouse inside a shield, negative-space stripes winding up the tower so the shield colour shows through them — there is no separate stripe ink. Five colourways, each tied to a specific ground.

  • Primary
    Sky shield · Coral heart

  • Dark
    Sky-lt shield · near-white heart

  • One-colour
    Navy only — letterhead, stamps

  • Reversed
    Paper shield · Cyan heart

  • Deep-tone·alt
    Muted-navy shield · Coral heart

Size & spacing

  • Full mark: 20px minimum, 28px+ recommended
  • Clear space: 12 units — 10% of the mark's width — on all sides
  • Below ~28px the stripes close up — use a favicon variant instead, never the full mark

Favicons are a different drawing

Not a scaled-down full mark. The tower fills solid Navy with no stripes, and the lighthouse is scaled 1.625×. Correct for 16–48px only; use the full mark above that.

Production notes

Rasterising the mark yourself

Use a real browser engine — Chromium, resvg, or Inkscape. cairosvg silently drops <mask> elements and returns a blank shield with no warning. The marks themselves are now flat filled paths rather than masks, but anything else in the identity that still uses one is at risk.

There is no stripe colour

The tower's helical bands are negative space — the shield fill shows through them. Any spec that names a separate "stripe ink" is wrong and out of date.
